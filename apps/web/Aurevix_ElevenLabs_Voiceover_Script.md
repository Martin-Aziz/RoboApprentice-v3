## Full Script

Aurevix helps teams teach industrial robots from a human demonstration.

Instead of starting with manual robot programming, an operator records the task the way it is already done on the floor.

In this example, we start with a multi-task robot demonstration.

Aurevix turns that demonstration into a structured workflow: task steps, sequence gates, robot waypoints, and a replayable motion policy.

The operator can review the generated sequence, adjust steps, and see the robot policy before anything is deployed.

This is the core shift:

Traditional robot programming can take days to weeks, and usually depends on a specialist engineer.

With Aurevix, the goal is to move from days to weeks down to minutes, using a workflow that ordinary production teams can understand.

Once the task is interpreted, Aurevix packages the result into formats teams can actually use.

The same workflow can generate URScript, ROS 2 Python, and a PDF summary for review, documentation, or handoff.

That means the demonstration is not just a video. It becomes a reusable robot skill.

Next, Aurevix helps build the business case.

Before a team commits to a new automation project, they can model the assumptions: how long programming takes today, how often tasks change, and what a faster teaching process could save.

The ROI view turns those assumptions into a shareable analysis, including hours saved, payback period, and projected savings.

So the team can discuss the pilot with both the engineering workflow and the business case in one place.

Aurevix brings the teaching workflow, generated robot code, and ROI story together.

From one demonstration to a reusable robot skill.
